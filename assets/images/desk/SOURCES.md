# Desk assets

Created on 2026-10-22 with the built-in imagegen tool. No April Zhu website assets were copied.

- oak-v2.png: refined original smoked oak desk, stitched sage-grey leather blotter, brass pen and ginkgo at the edges, natural side light. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool after the owner requested a more elegant treatment; no text or borrowed reference assets.
- objects.png: original transparent 3-column, 2-row atlas: research notebook, laptop, controller, photo frames, journal, folder. Decorative navigation objects, not research or product evidence. No labels; incidental keyboard markings may be present.
- research-v3.png: separately generated transparent premium sage research notebook and pencil. Unlabelled diagrams are decorative illustrations, not research results.
- game-v3.png: separately generated transparent graphite controller and conceptual game-design sketch. Original unbranded object, not a screenshot of the actual game.
- blog-v3.png: separately generated transparent burgundy personal journal, ribbon and fountain pen. Writing marks are decorative, not excerpts from the owner's articles.

The three v3 objects were generated individually with built-in imagegen after the owner requested higher-quality replacements. Prompts specify strict overhead photography, realistic tactile materials, upper-left natural light, full transparent padding and no legible text or branding. They replace only research, game and blog imagery in the preview; other objects retain the atlas.

The homepage overlays existing authorized hobby-photography.jpg and the real gamelibrary.webp screenshot. Text uses HTML. The owner approved the composition before production integration.

Production: scripts/prepare_desk_assets.py encodes the approved composition as WebP without cropping. Only *.webp files in this directory are mounted as public static assets. Backgrounds are 960/1600px; atlas 1280px; individual objects 640px. PNG source art and this note are not deployed.
